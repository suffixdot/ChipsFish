Hello! This is ChipsFish, the first serious damaths engine that can actually do things :)

Simply open start_gui.command, and it'll get straight to working

I believe this engine is extremely competitive, seeing so far it makes moves that seem useless, or even bad at first sight, but 5 moves later, turns out that very bad move had set up a sequence of moves that COOK you immediately!

You can turn on "Show Best Move" to understand the best lines and the best way to play against whatever.

The existence of this engine could potentially turn Damaths more strategic than mathematics-based, but its whatever lol

I also believe this should be unbeatable by any human at Depth 15, even at depth 3 it's already crazy, this should be able to solve Damaths like any other board game under a few months of constant calculating, but errrrrr I am not gonna keep my laptop up calculating all the time 

It has a feature where it remembers the best move for certain positions, so it doesn't constantly re-calculate what it already has, so theoretically, if you use this for a long time, you could be the one with a complete "book", all best moves in all positions.
